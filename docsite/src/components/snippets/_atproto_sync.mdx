<DetailsAdmo summary="Sync Status">

The **Synced** status found on the {props.name} Client's details page let's you know if if your **PDS History** is succesfully mirrored in the database. You can manually trigger a sync of the Mirror by using the **Sync Plays** action in the Client menu:

* **Full Sync** - reruns importing the 30 day/full scrobble history from your CAR file into the Mirror
* **Recent Sync** -- Uses the API to re-import the last ~100 scrobbles into the Mirror, to fill in any gaps in recent listening history
